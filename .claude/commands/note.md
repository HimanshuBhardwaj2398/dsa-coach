Note: $ARGUMENTS

1. If the note is empty, ask him what he noticed, in one line.
2. Run `python3 skills/dsa-coach/scripts/coach.py log feedback --text "<the note, in his words>"`. Add `--area` when it is obvious: coach, schedule, cli, app, panel, pane, explainer, content or docs.
3. Reply with the id it prints, in one line, and carry on with what we were doing.

Don't discuss the note, and don't act on it now. Notes are triaged between versions, in `plans/backlog.md`.
