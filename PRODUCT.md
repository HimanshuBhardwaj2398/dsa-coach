# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Himanshu, one learner preparing for coding interviews over a 90-day plan. He opens the study app (`python3 app/server.py`) on his laptop to work one problem at a time through the coach's eight-stage solve protocol, with the coach in a separate chat window. Sessions run from a single focus block to an evening. He is used to LeetCode- and NeetCode-style workspaces.

## Product Purpose

A local workspace for one solve: read the statement, answer the current stage's question, write and run the code, and hand the coach what it needs to check and log the attempt. Success means he spends his attention on the problem and the stage question, not on the page; nothing he types is ever lost; and the files stay honest (what he sees is what the coach and `python3 file.py` see).

## Brand Personality

Practice-site familiar: a dense two-pane workspace he already knows how to use, made quieter. Plain, precise, unhurried. The current stage's question is the most important text on screen; status (timer, hint rung, save state) is present but muted until it matters.

## Anti-references

- Busy practice sites: ads, sidebars, badges, every panel competing for attention.
- Heavy IDE chrome: VS Code-style activity bars, icon toolbars, panels everywhere.
- Generic SaaS: cards on cards, gradients, hero metrics.
- Copying any practice site's branding, colours or styling.

## Design Principles

1. **Show what this moment needs.** One stage, one statement, the code he is writing; the rest is a click away, never deleted.
2. **Familiar over clever.** Tabs, two panes, a test console: standard affordances he already reads without thinking.
3. **The file stays honest.** Hide by folding, never by keeping a second copy.
4. **Nothing is lost.** Unsaved text, conflicts and ended solves are always surfaced with a way out.
5. **The coach judges.** The page collects his answers and hands them over; it does not grade or log.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA contrast in both light and dark themes (the theme follows the system setting). Everything works by keyboard with visible focus. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Pass/fail is never shown by colour alone (text or symbol too).
